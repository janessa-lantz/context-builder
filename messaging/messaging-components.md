# Messaging Components

The building blocks of a company's messaging: what it believes, what it does, who it's for,
how it proves its claims, and how it's priced.

Define each component once, keep it current, and every piece of customer-facing work (and
every AI tool you point at it) draws from the same source of truth.

There are 26 components across 5 groups. Each has a stable **ID**, shown in `code`. The ID is
the handle downstream assets and AI tools use to reference the component.

---

## Who We Are

**Point of View** `point-of-view`
Our thesis about what is changing in the world and why it means our success is inevitable.

**Narrative** `narrative`
Why now, why us, what becomes possible today that wasn't before.

**Positioning** `positioning`
Where we sit in the market: what we do, who we do it for, and why we do it better.

**Founder Story** `founder-story`
Why the founders started the company and what they uniquely saw.

**Lexicon** `lexicon`
Terms and definitions, key concepts, banned terms.

**Company Description** `company-description`
Company boilerplate: 200 words, 100 words, 50 words, tagline, one-liner (for speaking),
elevator pitch (for speaking).

---

## What We Do

**Category Name** `category-name`
What it is.

**Product** `product`
The official proper nouns the company uses to name what it offers. A company may have several
products, each with its own capabilities.

**Capabilities** `capabilities`
What the product can do.

**Benefits** `benefits`
What the customer gains.

**Differentiators** `differentiators`
How the product differs from a given competitive alternative.

**How It Works** `how-it-works`
The technical details of the product and / or the customer's workflow using the product.

**Ecosystem & Integrations** `ecosystem-integrations`
The platforms, tools, and partners the product connects to: named integrations, supported
platforms, and SDKs, grouped by type.

---

## Who It's For

**Account** `account`
The ideal company we sell to: firmographics, technographics, growth signals.

**Buying Committee** `buying-committee`
Champion, economic buyer, end user, other titles mentioned.

**Jobs to Be Done** `jobs-to-be-done`
What the customer is trying to do. Per JTBD: level (business outcome / initiative / use
case), current approach, competitive alternatives.

**Value Drivers** `value-drivers`
Business outcomes this person is trying to achieve (revenue generation, efficiency, cost
savings, risk mitigation).

**Objections** `objections`
Common objections, concerns.

---

## Proof

**Customer Proof** `customer-proof`
Owned assets approved with customers: case studies, logos, quotes, references.

**Market Proof** `market-proof`
Institutional recognition: analysts, awards, press.

**Social Proof** `social-proof`
Validation found online: reviews, mentions, ratings.

**Key Metrics** `key-metrics`
Company momentum: number of customers, revenue, money raised, users.

---

## Pricing

**Pricing Model** `pricing-model`
Per-seat / usage / flat / hybrid.

**Packaging** `packaging`
Editions (starter, pro, enterprise) and what's in each.

**Add-ons & Services** `add-ons-services`
Premium support, implementation.

**Offers** `offers`
Free tier or trial and what's included.
