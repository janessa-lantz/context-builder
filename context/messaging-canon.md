# Messaging Canon

Your company's approved messaging: the rows with `status: canon` in the
[claims register](claims.md). The register is the source of truth; this page explains how the
canon is organized and read.

The canon is viewed by group, across six files. Each is a **compiled view** of the register:
regenerated by the [analyzer](../builder/jobs/analyzer.md) whenever the map recompiles, never
edited by hand. To change the canon, change the claims: mint or promote a claim in the
register, or add a `source: human` row. The views follow.

- [Who We Are](canon-who-we-are.md): point-of-view, narrative, positioning, founder-story, company-description
- [What We Do](canon-what-we-do.md): category-name, products, capabilities, features, unique-attributes, value-proposition, how-it-works, ecosystem-integrations
- [Who It's For](canon-who-its-for.md): icp, buying-committee, value-drivers
- [Proof](canon-proof.md): customer-proof, market-proof, key-metrics
- [Pricing](canon-pricing.md): pricing-model, packaging, add-ons-services, offers
- [Themes](canon-themes.md): themes, topics, campaigns

Voice, guardrails, and lexicon live in [brand-writing-identity.md](brand-writing-identity.md).
Visual conventions live in [brand-visual-identity.md](brand-visual-identity.md).

## How an entry renders

A compiled entry reads the way an analyst reports evidence: labeled bullets ranking the
claims, each traceable to the register by ID, then any human synthesis as prose. An example:

> ### positioning
>
> - Primary: "Ship quality AI at scale" (homepage hero) · clm-001
> - Variant, candidate: "The AI quality platform for production teams" (blog) · clm-003
>
> Lead with quality, not observability; the category label is for the nav and analysts, not
> the hero.
> clm-004 · source: human, 2026-07-07

What the example shows:

- **Quoted text is verbatim** from a live page, with the page noted and the claim ID at the
  end of the line. Every line traces to one register row.
- **Labeled bullets rank the claims.** The primary is the canonical phrasing; variants appear
  with their status for awareness, but only `canon` rows are approved messaging.
- **A plain paragraph is a `source: human` claim rendered as prose.** The LLM never edits or
  overwrites the underlying row.
- **Absence is written down** ("no canon claims yet"), never padded over.
