# Brand Writing Identity

How the company sounds — the verbal counterpart to [brand-visual-identity.md](brand-visual-identity.md). Skills read both so every generated asset sounds and looks like the company. Entries are verbatim from a live surface (source noted) or human synthesis (`source: human`); the LLM never overwrites a human-authored entry.

## Voice
*(the positive style: rhythm, posture, point of view, sentence shape, what to lead with and what to avoid leading with)*


## Guardrails
*(absolute, testable hard gates every output must pass — not preferences: banned phrases and claims, legal and compliance rules, trademark and naming rules, and things never said about competitors or customers. State each as a rule, with the correct behavior where there is one.)*


## Lexicon
*(coined or redefined terms, key concepts, and banned terms — term: definition or usage rule)*
