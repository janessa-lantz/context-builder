# One-Pager Formats

Reference for [artifact-one-pager](SKILL.md). Four canonical formats with section
allocations, visual hierarchy principles, and copy density limits.

## Format 1: Product

The default. Sales-assisted cycles, outbound attachments, event leave-behinds.

| Section | Share of page |
|---|---|
| Logo + category label | 10% |
| Hero H1 (outcome) + subhead | 25% |
| Problem statement | 15% |
| 3 benefits, side by side | 30% |
| Proof band: logo bar, quote, stat | 15% |
| CTA / URL / QR | 5% |

## Format 2: Solution Brief

Consultative cycles where the buyer has not categorized their problem yet. More narrative,
less product-forward.

| Section | Share of page |
|---|---|
| Logo + solution label | 10% |
| Hero H1 (use-case outcome) | 20% |
| Use-case vignette (a day-in-the-life moment from `jobs-to-be-done`) | 20% |
| Capability grid (6 items, 2 columns) | 30% |
| Outcomes: 3 quantified metrics | 10% |
| Proof + CTA | 10% |

## Format 3: Integration

Partner launches, joint marketing, channel enablement.

| Section | Share of page |
|---|---|
| Both logos | 15% |
| Hero H1 ("Together, ...") | 20% |
| Why this integration matters | 15% |
| Joint benefits (3) | 25% |
| Setup in 3 steps | 15% |
| Proof + CTA | 10% |

## Format 4: Vertical

Vertical GTM plays ("for healthcare," "for fintech").

| Section | Share of page |
|---|---|
| Logo + "for [industry]" label | 10% |
| Hero H1 (industry-framed) + subhead | 25% |
| Industry pains (3, specific) | 25% |
| Industry-specific benefits (3) | 25% |
| Industry-specific proof + CTA | 15% |

## Visual hierarchy principles

1. **One clear winner per page.** The hero H1 is the largest element by at least 2x.
2. **Three tiers of type.** H1, section headers, body. Five or more sizes reads chaotic.
3. **Grid is king.** Align to a 12-column grid; broken grids read amateur.
4. **Color restraint.** Primary on logo and H1, accent only on CTA and the hero stat, everything else neutral.
5. **White space is structural.** Quarter to half-inch margins minimum; divide sections with space, not lines.
6. **Customer logos in grayscale**, so the proof band reads as trust, not a competing palette.

## Copy density limits

Enforce strictly. If the human wants more words, push back.

| Element | Max words | Ideal |
|---|---|---|
| Hero H1 | 10 | 6 to 8 |
| Subhead | 25 | 15 to 20 |
| Problem statement | 50 | 25 to 35 |
| Benefit headline | 6 | 3 to 4 |
| Benefit body | 30 | 15 to 20 |
| Section header | 8 | 4 to 6 |
| Pull quote | 40 | 20 to 30 |
| CTA | 5 | 2 to 3 |

Any section over 60 words of body copy gets split or cut in half.

## Anti-patterns

- More than 3 benefits
- Hedged heroes ("helps you improve...")
- Stock art; use brand graphics or product screenshot crops instead
- Full paragraphs inside benefit grids
- Mixed icon styles (pick outlined, filled, or hand-drawn; never combine)
- Logo bars with more than 7 logos
