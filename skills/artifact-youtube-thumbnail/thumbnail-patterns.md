# Thumbnail Patterns

Reference for [artifact-youtube-thumbnail](SKILL.md). CTR-tested patterns for B2B and
educational YouTube, color guidance, text rules, and the legibility test.

## Five patterns

Consumer-style extremes (exaggerated faces, maximum contrast) do not transfer to B2B; the
audience self-selects differently. These five do:

1. **Face + big text.** A face on one side (40 to 60% of frame, direct eye contact, a real expression), 2 to 3 words opposite at the eye line. For creator-led, interview, and opinion content. Never center the face; centered reads as a stock portrait.
2. **Stat shock.** One number fills 40 to 60% of the frame, minimal supporting elements. For data and research content, and contrarian takes with a number behind them. The number must be real (from `key-metrics` or cited research).
3. **Before/after split.** Desaturated chaos on one side, branded order on the other. For transformation and how-to content where the change is visual.
4. **Object + annotation.** A product screenshot crop with one arrow or circle and a 2-word callout. For walkthroughs and "the feature you missed" content.
5. **Meme-format reframe.** A recognizable meme structure applied to the topic. High share potential, high cringe risk; only with full cultural fluency and an audience that gets it.

## Color guidance

Thumbnails fight for attention at 300px wide, so push saturation and contrast past the
brand's web norms.

| Tone | Signal | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| Red | urgency, warning | stat shock, contrarian, "stop doing X" |
| Orange | energy, action | high-energy how-to, transformation |
| Navy | authority, trust | research, frameworks, deep dives |
| Green | growth, results | outcome-led content |
| Black | premium, editorial | opinion, thought leadership |
| Brand primary | consistency | tutorials, channel-identity videos |

Avoid muddy palettes, pure white backgrounds (they vanish into YouTube's UI), and gradients.

## Text rules

- 4 words maximum; 2 to 3 ideal
- Minimum size at 1280x720: 80pt for 4 words, 100pt for 2 to 3, 120pt+ for 1
- Bold sans-serif, tight letter spacing; all caps usually reads better at small sizes
- Grayscale test: if the text disappears in grayscale, contrast is too low

## The 300px test

Scale the finished thumbnail to 300px wide (mobile preview size) and check: the text reads,
the focal point is clear, and it stands out against a white feed. Fail any one and go back.

## Pattern interrupt

The eye tracks the biggest thing on screen first; direct eye contact grabs attention;
off-center composition breaks the grid of centered thumbnails; and a color break against the
surrounding feed (light in a dark field, warm in a blue-heavy tech field) does the rest.
Extreme cleanliness in a cluttered field is also an interrupt; restraint can win.

## Anti-patterns

- Stock photography
- Emoji (amateur signal for B2B)
- The logo as a dominant element; the channel avatar already shows it
- Watermark strips along the bottom
- Small "depth" text nobody will zoom into
- Blurry source imagery; YouTube compresses, start sharp
